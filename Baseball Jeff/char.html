Meet the Characters!

Baseball Jeff

The star of the show! Jeff loves baseball and always wears his signature shirt with red stitches. He’s cheerful, determined, and the glue that holds the gang together.

Tennis Carl

Carl is Jeff’s best friend, but he’s complicated: often mean, sometimes nice, greedy, and a total self‑glazer. Despite his flaws, he and Jeff share a strong bond and stick together through every adventure.

Extra Friends

Other quirky characters who join Jeff and Carl in their fun animations, each bringing their own chaos and charm.
